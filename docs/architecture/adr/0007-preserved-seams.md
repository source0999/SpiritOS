# ADR 0007: Preserve proven operating seams

Decision: retain the canonical context broker, model/LiteLLM seam, approval/Undo/receipt policies, deterministic anti-cheat principles, and Jellyfin deployment boundary.
Reason: Campaign 1 repairs ownership without replacing proven product behavior.
Consequences: narrow adapters and contracts are allowed; broad rewrites are not.
Campaign 2 dependency: decomposition plans must preserve these seams.
Revision condition: characterization and independent verification prove a seam is unsafe.
